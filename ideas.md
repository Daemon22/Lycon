# Lycon Website Direction

## Ground-truth brief

Create a reliable, functional browser-style Lycon preview that preserves the established product language: a dark field-instrument shell, a large cinematic environment, an unmistakable wolf mark, and a clear distinction between Public / Online and Private / Proven modes. The website must render its branded imagery through stable project-managed asset URLs rather than fragile external references.

## Chosen Approach: Quiet Field Instrument

**Design Movement:** Contemporary editorial software design blended with field-journal instrumentation and cinematic environmental photography.

**Core Principles:** The interface uses an asymmetric browser frame instead of a centered marketing layout; every control should feel purposeful and tactile; photography carries the emotional weight while overlays preserve legibility; private and public states are distinct but belong to one coherent system.

**Color Philosophy:** Public mode is forest charcoal with electric blue signal accents, expressing reach, clarity, and connection. Private mode is cave charcoal with muted amber accents, expressing shelter, focus, and control. Neutral surfaces stay near-black so the imagery and mode color remain the visual hierarchy.

**Layout Paradigm:** A browser shell dominates the viewport, with a compact sidebar and instrument-like toolbar framing a generous hero field. Hero copy anchors to the left while the environment opens toward the right, leaving room for the product story to breathe without a generic centered-card composition.

**Signature Elements:** A square wolf mark in the upper-left navigation; a segmented mode switch with a colored signal dot; thin measurement-like eyebrow labels and status chips that make the page feel like a working instrument.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Mode switching is immediate, legible, and reversible. Hover states reveal a little more signal without adding noise. Buttons should feel like controls on a physical console, with restrained press feedback and no ornamental motion that competes with the hero.

**Animation:** Use short opacity and transform transitions under 240ms. The hero enters with a small upward lift, mode changes crossfade the environment and tint, and status dots may pulse slowly only when communicating an active state. Respect reduced-motion preferences by removing nonessential movement.

**Typography System:** Use Space Grotesk for the display voice and IBM Plex Mono for instrument labels, metadata, and status readouts. Headlines are large, tight, and slightly tracked negative; utility text is compact, uppercase, and letter-spaced.

**Brand Essence:** Lycon is a browser for people who want the local web to feel calm and the wider web to feel deliberate; it is different because privacy and connection are presented as two visible operating states rather than hidden settings. Personality: grounded, watchful, independent.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are concise and evocative, while CTAs are direct and specific. Example lines: “Browse wild. Browse free.” and “Keep the signal on your terms.” Avoid generic welcome language.

**Wordmark & Logo:** Use the supplied wolf emblem as the primary mark, cropped into a controlled upper-left badge and repeated only where a browser identity cue is necessary. Do not substitute a default text logo.

**Signature Brand Color:** Signal Blue `#4b8dff` for Public / Online mode, paired with Proven Amber `#d59a3f` for Private / Proven mode.

## Style Decisions

The wolf emblem is treated as a dark controlled instrument plate rather than a floating marketing lockup. Public and Private are presented as a paired operating system in the mode ribbon, with blue and amber state chips visible together. Hero identity is carried by calibrated readouts, status cards, mono labels, and the browser frame instead of a decorative serif wordmark.
